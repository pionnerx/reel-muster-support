Player support

Get help with your first defender, saved voyages, or a problem on your iPhone.

Report a problem

How do I recruit my first defender?

Pull the lever on the preparation screen. Three matching symbols on a winning line summon a defender. Tap their portrait to see their abilities, then start the battle.

How do I replay a cleared route?

Open the sea chart and select a harbor and difficulty you have already cleared. Choose Autoplay to let your crew recruit and fight through each wave. You can take control again during the voyage.

How do I continue a voyage?

Reopen the game on the same iPhone to continue your voyage. Progress is saved on the device. If you are reporting a crash, keep the app installed to preserve your save.
