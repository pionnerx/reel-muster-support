Privacy policy

This policy covers the free iOS edition of Reel Muster (摇出个军团). You can play offline without an account.

Game data on your device

Your device stores your current voyage, recruits, equipment, game currency, completed routes, and preferences such as language and sound. The game uses this information to resume play and apply the settings you choose. The developer does not receive your save or battle history. The game has no advertising, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases.

Data retention and deletion

Local progress stays on your device between sessions. To remove the app and its local save, choose Delete App in iPhone Settings. Starting another voyage keeps your permanent progression. Device backups, when enabled, are controlled through your Apple settings.

Contact and support

If you contact the team using the support tracker, GitHub publishes the report and your GitHub username. We use the information you choose to share to answer you and investigate the issue. Keep private details out of public reports. You can edit your report on GitHub or ask the team to remove it.

GitHub hosts this support website and logs IP addresses of site visitors for security. For its retention and data choices, see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

简体中文 · 隐私说明

本说明适用于《摇出个军团》iOS 免费版。游戏可以离线游玩，无需注册账号。

游戏数据与删除

当前设备保存航线、角色、装备、游戏货币、通关记录和语言、音效等偏好，用于继续游玩。开发者不会收到玩家的存档或战斗记录。游戏没有广告、分析、跟踪或内购。要清除这台设备上的游戏数据，请在 iPhone 设置中选择“删除 App”。重新开始航行会保留永久进度。设备备份由你的苹果设置管理。

联系开发团队

通过公开支持区反馈时，GitHub 会公开你的用户名和反馈内容。开发团队使用你自愿提供的信息来答复和排查问题。请避免在公开反馈中填写个人信息。你可以编辑自己的反馈，或联系开发团队删除。GitHub 托管此支持页面并出于安全目的记录访问者的 IP 地址。详见GitHub 隐私声明。
