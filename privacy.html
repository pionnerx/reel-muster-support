Privacy policy

Emberwake Legion is a free iOS game. You can play offline without creating an account.

What the game saves

Your iPhone stores your current voyage, defenders, equipment, game currency, completed routes, language, and sound settings. The game reads these records to resume your voyage and apply your preferences.

The developer does not receive your save or battle history. The free iOS game includes no ads, analytics, tracking, or in-app purchases.

Keeping or deleting your progress

Your progress stays on the device between sessions. Starting another voyage keeps your permanent progress. To erase the app and its local save, choose Delete App in iPhone Settings. If you enable device backups, you manage them through your Apple settings.

What happens when you submit a report

If you submit a support request on GitHub, your report and GitHub username are public. We use the details you share to answer your question and investigate the issue. Keep personal information out of public reports.

You can edit your report on GitHub or ask the team to remove it.
